How It Works

1. Getting Started (You, the Homemaker)

  • Sign up with your business name, phone, email, and a password.
  • Your account waits for approval, usually within 24 hours — you'll get an email once you're approved.
  • Log in, then go to Menu Manager and add your food items with price, unit (e.g. "per box"), and an optional tag like "Veg" or "Eggless".
  • Go to Settings and fill in your UPI ID and payee name, choose your own custom link, and optionally upload your business logo.

2. The Link You Share With Customers

Your personal order link is shown at the top of the Settings page, along with a QR code you can share directly. It looks like:

homebites.anyapps.in/your-chosen-name

Send this link to your customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, or anywhere else — every order placed through it comes straight to your dashboard.

3. How a Customer Orders

  • They open your link, enter their phone number (this remembers their name and address for next time).
  • They fill in or confirm their delivery address.
  • They browse your menu and choose quantities of what they want.
  • They place the order — it appears on your dashboard under Pending.

4. Getting Paid — the QR Code is Automatic

You don't need to create or upload any QR code yourself. The moment you save your UPI ID and payee name in Settings, HomeBites generates a fresh QR code for every single order automatically — already filled in with the exact order amount. The customer just scans and pays, or you can mark the order as paid by Cash instead.

5. Managing Orders

  • Pending — new orders waiting for you to confirm payment (UPI or Cash), or cancel.
  • Confirmed — paid orders waiting to be delivered. Tap Mark Delivered once done.
  • Phone Order — for orders you take yourself over a phone call. No payment step, just a quick record of who ordered what, for your own records.
  • Reports — pick any date range to see totals by item (how much of each dish sold) and by person (how much each customer ordered).

6. Pricing

Every new account gets one month free. After that, it's ₹199/month or ₹2000/year. Contact vkvcoder.support@gmail.com to continue, or with any questions at all.
